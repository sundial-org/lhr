What we study

Can a model learn your judgment from working with you?

Every edit, comment, acceptance and reversal is a trace of human judgment. Nothing like it exists on the internet, because it was never written down anywhere. We think the next models will learn from traces like these.

We work on three questions. What the best surface for thinking looks like when machines do most of the drafting. Whether a model can learn from collaboration when to act, when to ask, and when to leave things alone. And how several people and several agents can share one document without losing track of who did what.

We are a few people, hiring researchers and engineers in personalization, reinforcement learning and interfaces. If our work sounds interesting to you, write to us at team@longhorizonresearch.com.
